Privacy Policy for MyWorker Thailand

Effective date: 20 October 2026
Application: MyWorker Thailand (com.myworkerthailand.app)
Contact: myworkerthailand@gmail.com

MyWorker Thailand is an independent information and assistance application for Myanmar workers in Thailand. It is not operated, affiliated with, or endorsed by the Thai government. It does not issue visas or work permits or make official legal decisions.

1. Information you enter

The app is designed to store information you choose to enter, such as document types and expiry dates, reminder settings, and supported worker-related records, locally on your device. A MyWorker account is not required for the core functions. The app is not designed to upload these personal records to a MyWorker cloud account.

2. Location and nearby help

With your permission, the app may access your device's approximate or precise location to show nearby help information and support emergency location assistance. You can decline or change location permissions in Android settings. When you choose to open maps or share your location using another application, that external application or service may receive location or other information necessary to perform your request, under its own privacy practices. MyWorker does not intend to retain a server-side history of your GPS locations.

3. Translation and internet-dependent services

The current Android release supports online text translation between Myanmar, Thai, and English. When you choose to translate text, the text you submit and the source and target language selections are transmitted over the internet to the MyWorker translation gateway hosted on Microsoft Azure. The gateway forwards the translation request to an external translation provider and returns the translated result to the app. The text must be processed to provide the requested translation. MyWorker is designed not to store raw translation text as a server-side translation history. This does not mean that the hosting platform or translation provider processes no operational or security data; their processing and retention practices may differ. Please avoid entering passwords, financial credentials, or other highly sensitive information into online translation.

Live nationwide nearby-help lookup is not yet a completed feature. Availability of other online services depends on the installed app version and configuration.

4. Notifications and reminders

If you allow notifications, the app can schedule reminders for document dates using Android notification services. Delivery can be affected by device settings, battery management, and operating-system restrictions. Notifications are an aid, not a guarantee of compliance with official deadlines. Verify legal deadlines with the relevant government office.

5. External applications and links

When you choose to call an office, open a website, open a map, or share information, Android or the external service you select may process information according to its own privacy policy. MyWorker does not control those third-party services.

6. Storage, backup, and deletion

Personal records are intended to remain on your device. Android automatic backup is disabled in the verified release configuration. You can remove supported records using available app controls or clear the app's data through Android settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may permanently remove locally stored records. Do not assume that reinstalling will restore them. Any optional manual import or restore capability should only be relied upon if explicitly available and verified in your installed version.

Because these records are stored locally, the developer cannot remotely delete them from your device. For privacy questions or concerns about information processed through MyWorker's online services, contact myworkerthailand@gmail.com. Please identify the service involved without sending passwords, passport images, or other sensitive records. Data processed by external providers may be subject to their own retention and deletion procedures. This contact address is not a guarantee that data held by an independent provider can be deleted by MyWorker.

7. Data security and third parties

We aim to minimize unnecessary handling of personal data. The online translation feature uses an HTTPS connection to the MyWorker gateway. Other data transfers, including transfers you initiate to external apps or websites, may be handled by those services under their own privacy and security practices. We do not claim that third parties never receive data or that all operational metadata is immediately deleted. Material changes in the app's data handling will be reflected in this policy.

8. Children

The app is primarily designed for workers and is not specifically directed to children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's functions, service providers, or data practices change. The updated policy and its effective date will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, concerns about online data handling, or inquiries about data deletion, contact myworkerthailand@gmail.com. Please do not email sensitive identity documents or passwords. We will review inquiries relating to MyWorker-controlled services; requests involving independent third-party providers may need to be directed to those providers.
